Perch Home

Home dashboard for iPad

Perch Home turns an iPad into an always-on household display for weather, calendars, photos, Apple Home devices, cameras, and guest Wi-Fi.

What it does

  • Weather and timeCurrent conditions, forecast details, and a readable household clock.
  • Calendar eventsApple Calendar and Google Calendar events shown at a glance.
  • Photo slideshowsPersonal photo library slideshows that stay on the device.
  • Apple HomeHomeKit device status and Apple Home camera views for supported homes.
  • Guest Wi-FiVisitor-friendly network details displayed where your household needs them.
  • Simple setupTemplate-based onboarding for a focused always-on display.

Privacy

Perch Home is designed for personal household use. Most data stays on your device. Calendar, weather, analytics, and crash-reporting services are used only where needed to provide app features and improve reliability.
