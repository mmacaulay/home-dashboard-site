Perch Home

Privacy Policy - Last updated May 1, 2026

Perch Home is a personal iPad kiosk app. This policy describes what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data accessed on your device

Perch Home accesses the following on your device, solely to display information on screen:

  • Apple Calendar - to show today's events
  • Photos - to display a slideshow from your photo library
  • Reminders - to display your shopping list
  • HomeKit - to show smart home device status and camera feeds
  • Local network - to connect to HomeKit devices on your network

None of this data is transmitted off your device by the app.

Third-party services

  • Google Calendar - if you choose to connect a Google account, the app uses the Google Calendar API (read-only scope) to fetch your calendar events for display on screen. This requires signing in with Google and authorises read-only access to your calendar events. Calendar data is fetched directly from Google's servers and is not stored or transmitted anywhere else. Perch Home's use of Google Calendar data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple WeatherKit - your configured location (latitude and longitude) is sent to Apple's WeatherKit API to fetch weather data. This is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • TelemetryDeck - the app sends anonymous, aggregate usage events (such as app launch, onboarding steps completed, and which features are active) to TelemetryDeck for product analytics. No personally identifiable information is collected. Device identifiers are hashed before transmission and cannot be reversed. This is governed by TelemetryDeck's Privacy Policy.

Data storage

App settings (weather location, Wi-Fi credentials, dashboard title) are stored locally on your device using iOS secure storage. No data is synced to the cloud or shared with any third party.
